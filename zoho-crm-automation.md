# Zoho CRM Automation

Canonical guide: https://atomation.io/zoho-crm-automation/

## Quick Answer

Zoho CRM automation connects lead sources, customer records, owner assignments, tasks, follow-up reminders, and reporting so small teams can keep Zoho accurate without manual CRM cleanup all day.

## Good First Automations

- Send website form submissions into Zoho CRM.
- Assign lead owners based on service type, location, or source.
- Create follow-up tasks when a lead enters a stage.
- Send owner alerts for high-priority inquiries.
- Sync reporting rows into Google Sheets.
- Connect invoice or payment status from accounting tools.

## Fields to Map

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Lead source
- Service requested
- Owner
- Stage
- Follow-up date
- Notes

## Reliability Notes

Zoho automations should include duplicate checks, clear required fields, visible error alerts, and a defined source of truth. The goal is not to move every field everywhere. The goal is to make follow-up faster and customer records cleaner.
