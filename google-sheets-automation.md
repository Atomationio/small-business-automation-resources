# Google Sheets Automation

Canonical guide: https://atomation.io/google-sheets-automation/

## Quick Answer

Google Sheets automation turns spreadsheet rows into workflows that capture form submissions, update CRMs, sync invoice status, send alerts, and create reports without manual copy-paste.

## Good First Automations

- Send website form submissions into a clean tracking sheet.
- Create CRM records from approved spreadsheet rows.
- Send an owner alert when a row needs action.
- Build a daily or weekly summary from sheet data.
- Update invoice or payment tracking from QuickBooks.

## Fields to Clean Up First

- Customer name
- Email and phone
- Lead source
- Service requested
- Status or stage
- Owner
- Follow-up date
- Notes

## Reliability Notes

Spreadsheets are flexible, but they can get messy fast. A good Google Sheets automation should validate required fields, avoid duplicate rows, make failed syncs visible, and define which app is the real source of truth.
