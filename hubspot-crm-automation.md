# HubSpot CRM Automation

Canonical guide: https://atomation.io/hubspot-crm-automation/

## Quick Answer

HubSpot CRM automation keeps contacts, lifecycle stages, tasks, owners, and follow-up workflows updated so small teams can respond faster and reduce manual CRM cleanup.

## Good First Automations

- Create or update contacts from website forms.
- Assign lead owners based on form answers or source.
- Create follow-up tasks after a new inquiry.
- Update lifecycle stages when a lead becomes qualified.
- Send deal or owner reminders.
- Sync reporting data into Google Sheets.

## Fields to Map

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Lead source
- Lifecycle stage
- Owner
- Deal stage
- Follow-up date
- Notes

## Reliability Notes

HubSpot automations should include duplicate handling, clean lifecycle stage rules, field validation, and visible alerts when a workflow cannot update a contact or deal. The goal is a CRM the team trusts, not more manual cleanup.
