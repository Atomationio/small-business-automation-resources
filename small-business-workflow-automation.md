# Small Business Workflow Automation Guide

Canonical guide: https://atomation.io/small-business-workflow-automation/

Small business workflow automation is the process of turning repeatable manual steps into connected systems. Good candidates include lead intake, CRM updates, invoice follow-up, scheduling, review requests, reporting, and spreadsheet maintenance.

## Quick answer

Start with workflows that happen every week, touch more than one app, and create delays or missed follow-up when handled manually. Map the trigger, required data, destination systems, exception handling, and owner before choosing tools.

## Common workflows to automate

- Website form submission -> CRM record -> owner alert -> follow-up task
- New invoice -> reminder sequence -> payment status update -> spreadsheet/report
- New customer -> onboarding email -> folder/task setup -> calendar reminder
- Completed job -> review request -> testimonial tracking -> follow-up note
- Weekly app data -> Google Sheets or dashboard -> status email

## Tools often involved

- WordPress, Gravity Forms, Jotform, or Google Forms
- Zoho CRM, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive, or Salesforce
- QuickBooks, Stripe, Square, or PayPal
- Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, or Microsoft 365
- n8n, Zapier, Make, APIs, and webhooks
