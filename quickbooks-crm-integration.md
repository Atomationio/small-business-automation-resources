# QuickBooks and CRM Integration Guide

Canonical guide: https://atomation.io/quickbooks-crm-integration/

QuickBooks and CRM integration helps small businesses reduce duplicate entry between customer records, invoices, payment status, follow-up tasks, and reporting.

## Quick answer

Connect QuickBooks to a CRM when invoice status, customer details, payment reminders, or sales follow-up need to stay aligned. The safest first workflow is usually syncing customer and invoice status into the CRM, then adding reminders or alerts after the data flow is reliable.

## Common integration workflows

- New CRM customer -> QuickBooks customer
- QuickBooks invoice created -> CRM note or deal update
- Invoice overdue -> follow-up task or reminder sequence
- Payment received -> CRM status update
- Monthly finance data -> Google Sheets or dashboard report

## Setup checklist

- Decide which system owns customer names, emails, and phone numbers.
- Confirm whether invoices, estimates, payments, or only statuses should sync.
- Map duplicate customer rules before syncing.
- Add logging and owner alerts for failed syncs.
- Test with a small set of customers before broad rollout.
